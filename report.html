Notes from the lab

Security reports.

Documenting the process, understanding the impact, and finding a path to remediation.

Metasploitable
The Metasploitable 1 lab revealed outdated services (Apache, vsftpd, Samba) and weak default credentials, allowing unauthorized access and potential data leakage. Remediation includes patching, disabling unused services, and enforcing strong passwords.
Kioptrix level 1
The Kioptrix Level 1 lab exposed vulnerable services and weak configurations that allow local privilege escalation and information disclosure. Recommended actions: patch vulnerable packages, tighten service configurations, and remove unnecessary accounts.
