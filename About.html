About

I’m Ahmed Mohamed, a Computer Engineering student at Modern Academy for Engineering and Technology with a strong passion for Cybersecurity, Ethical Hacking, and Network Security. I focus on offensive security, penetration testing, and red teaming, combining academic knowledge with practical experience gained through cybersecurity labs, CTF challenges, and real-world projects. I have hands-on experience with Linux environments, networking, security tools, and programming, continuously improving my skills in identifying vulnerabilities, securing systems, and building efficient solutions. I also work as a Mentor Intern at ADA, supporting learners with technical guidance, practical security concepts, and structured learning paths. Alongside that, I create cybersecurity content on YouTube through pwn_sec, focused on making offensive security topics easier to understand and apply.
